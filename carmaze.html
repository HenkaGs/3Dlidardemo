CarMaze

Each car is a small neural network (23 inputs, 12 hidden, 2 outputs: steering and throttle). It sees only 9 distance rays across a 180° view (forward, or backward while reversing), 9 matching finish-in-sight signals, its own speed and steering angle, and 3 numbers about the nearest junction sign it can see in its field of view (how close it is and which way its arrow points relative to the car). Every junction has a yellow arrow sign pointing along the shortest way to the finish. After each generation the best cars are bred with mutation. The path distance to the finish is used only to score a car after it drives; the car never sees the map. Crashing costs a large score penalty, stalling a smaller one, and reversing or flipping between forward and reverse also costs score. Seeing the finish zone earns a bonus that grows as the car gets closer. Red cars crashed, orange ones stalled; the leader's rays turn pink when reversing and green when they see the finish.
